Notes:

* This experiment changed only num.stream.threads from 1 to 2.
* Pod CPU and memory limits remained unchanged.
* With six partitions, at most six stream tasks could be active despite eight configured stream threads.
* Processor distribution was recorded per pod, not per individual stream thread.
* The experiment showed lower throughput than the one-thread-per-pod baseline under the tested resource limits.
* This result does not imply that additional stream threads are generally harmful.
